About

Majek Ay

Some people call him Intern. Some know him as erthxn. On the internet he answers to Earth Intern, but mostly he just watches.

Majek Ay doesn't do loud. He's the quiet one in the room reading the room before anyone else has noticed there's something to read. Community architecture is his craft, the invisible scaffolding that keeps a Telegram or Discord from tipping into chaos, but that's the work, not the whole story.

Off the clock, he's rewatching Basic Instinct (1992) for the twentieth time, still catching new detail in Nick Curran's unraveling. He'll tell you Peaky Blinders is less a show and more a study in how to move through a room without saying much and still own it. Somewhere in between, Rick and Morty keeps him honest, a reminder that chaos and control aren't opposites, they're neighbors.

He doesn't explain himself. He doesn't need to. Ask around, the work usually does the talking before he does.
