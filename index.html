What I build

Infrastructure for communities that actually hold up.

Every project needs something different — here's where most engagements start.

01

Community Architecture

Telegram and Discord servers designed from the ground up — role structures, channel logic, moderation systems, and onboarding flows that hold up as a community scales.

02

Engagement Systems

Custom bots and mechanics — XP, ranks, quests, gated access — built to keep a community active between announcements, not just during them.

03

Growth Infrastructure

Mini Apps and utility tools — gift scouting, price tracking, username sniping — that give a project something genuinely useful to point people toward.

How it works

Four stages, one operator.

01

Diagnose

Audit the current community, or the gap where one should be — where people are dropping off, and what's missing.

02

Architect

Design the structure: roles, channels, moderation logic, and the mechanics that will keep people coming back.

03

Launch

Build and ship — bots, servers, and assets that match the brand and are ready for real traffic on day one.

04

Operate

Moderate, iterate, and grow the community day to day — the part most agencies skip.
